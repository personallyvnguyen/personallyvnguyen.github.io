Privacy Policy

Grease Da Groove · Effective October 19, 2026

Grease Da Groove is a fitness log for workouts, runs, food and progress photos. There is no account to create, and we don't run a server that stores your data.

What stays on your phone

Everything you log stays on your device: workouts, runs (including the route of each run), food ratings, body ratings, measurements and progress photos. We never receive it.

  • Location is used only while you record a run, ruck or hike, to measure distance. The route is saved on your phone with that run.
  • Camera and photos are used only when you take or add a progress photo. Photos stay in the app on your phone. Location data is removed from photos you import.
  • Backups. On iPhone, a copy of your log (not photos) is kept in your own iCloud account so it can come back after a reinstall. Your phone's own iCloud or Google backup may also include the app's data. Backup files you export go wherever you choose to save them.

What is shared

  • Usage analytics. The app sends anonymous usage events (for example, which tab was opened) to Google Firebase Analytics so we can see which features are used. These include device and app details and an app-instance identifier, not your logged data. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Ads. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads are non-personalized: we don't ask to track you across other apps. AdMob still receives device and network information (such as IP address and device type) to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In some regions you'll be asked for consent first. Pro removes ads.
  • Purchases. Pro is billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details; the store only tells the app whether a subscription is active.

Google's handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone else.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your log stays on your phone until you delete it. You can clear any section in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything on the device. On iPhone, the iCloud copy of your log can be removed in iOS Settings under your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
